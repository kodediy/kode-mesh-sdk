mesh_aos
========

The owner: the one Active Object, AO_Mesh, that finds the module on the expansion header and drives its radio and its GNSS, and the rules it holds every transmit to (mesh_plan.c). The two layers ask it; an app does not include it.

Depends on: mesh_drv, mesh_bsp, and kdefs, kode_sys, kqp and klog from kode-sdk

Docs: https://docs.kode.diy/modules/mesh
